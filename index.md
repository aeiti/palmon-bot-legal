# Palmon Bot

An unofficial Discord bot for *Palmon: Survival* communities — Palmon and trait
lookups, event schedules, and gift codes.

This site hosts the bot's public policies:

- **[Terms of Service](terms-of-service/)** — what the bot is, what the game
  data is worth, and the rules for using it.
- **[Privacy Policy](privacy-policy/)** — what the bot stores (Discord ID
  numbers and your server's settings), what it does not, and how to have it
  removed.

## Supporting the bot

Palmon Bot is free to use and free to add to a server. If you would like to help
with what it costs to run, donations are accepted through **Buy Me a Coffee**, at
[buymeacoffee.com/palmontools](https://buymeacoffee.com/palmontools).

A donation is a gift, not a purchase. It unlocks nothing — no feature, no
priority, no perk — and every command stays available to every server either
way. Nothing is sold through the bot, and there is no paid tier.

[Section 10 of the Terms of Service](terms-of-service/#donations) sets out the
full terms, including recurring donations and how to get a refund;
[the Privacy Policy](privacy-policy/#donations) explains what the donation
platform holds and what the bot does not.

## About

Palmon Bot is a fan-made project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or
connected to the developers or publishers of *Palmon: Survival*.
