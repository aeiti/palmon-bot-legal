# Terms of Service

**Palmon Bot** — a Discord bot for *Palmon: Survival* communities.

Last updated: 25 September 2026

agree to these terms. If you do not agree with them, remove the bot from your
server and stop using its commands.

## 1. What the bot is

Palmon Bot answers questions about *Palmon: Survival* — Palmon stats, traits,
mounts, bosses, VIP perks — and tracks the game's recurring event schedules and
gift codes. It posts its answers in Discord and nowhere else.

It is a free hobby project. Every command is available to every server: there is
no paid tier, no subscription, and nothing is sold through it. Voluntary
donations towards its running costs are accepted, and they buy nothing — see
section 10.

## 3. The game data is community-compiled, and may be wrong

Everything the bot reports about the game — stat percentages, skill text, event
timings, reward tables — was compiled by hand from screenshots and in-game
observation. It is not read from any official source or API.

That means it can be **incomplete, out of date, or simply wrong**, especially
after a game update. Figures captured from a unit at one level do not
necessarily hold at another. Treat what the bot says as a helpful indication,
not as fact, and check anything that matters in the game itself. Decisions you
make in the game on the strength of the bot's output are your own.

## 4. Gift codes

Gift codes are picked up from public announcement posts and listed as-is. A
code may have already run out of redemptions, may be region-locked, may expire
sooner than its listed date, or may never have been valid. Listing a code is not
a promise that it works.

Do not use the bot to post codes you are not permitted to share.

restriction is enforced by Discord permissions, which your server's
administrators control. **It is your server's responsibility to grant those
permissions to the right people.** The bot honours whatever Discord tells it.

**A donation is a gift, not a purchase.** Nothing is supplied in return:

Buy Me a Coffee takes the payment and passes it on. Your card details are handled

**Recurring donations.** Nothing recurs unless you choose a recurring option on
Buy Me a Coffee yourself. If you do, you can change or cancel it at any time from
your account there, without asking anyone.

**Refunds.** A gift is not normally refundable. That said, mistakes happen: if

**Not a charity.** This is one person's hobby project, not a registered charity
